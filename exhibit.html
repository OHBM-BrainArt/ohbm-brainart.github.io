Challenging the Zeitgeist: The Next Neural Revolution

THE EXHIBITION SUBMISSION IS NOW CLOSED!

Challenging the Zeitgeist: The Next Neural Revolution

Challenging the Zeitgeist is intentionally open-ended and invites broad interpretation. We welcome a diversity of 2D and 3D submissions engaging with neuroscience, artistic practice, and the forces that shape how knowledge is produced, challenged, and transformed.

The exhibition will be held at the Organization for Human Brain Mapping (OHBM) Annual Meeting between June 14-18 2026, in Bordeaux, France. Artists and scientists were invited to submit their proposals for the exhibition in any form (still images, videos, sculptures, written word, interactive displays).


Important dates:

  • Proposal submission opening: January 4, 2026
  • Proposal submission deadline: March 15, 2026
  • Announcement of selected proposals: TBA, April, 2026.
