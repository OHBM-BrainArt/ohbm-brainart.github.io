2026 BrainArt Kids Live Activities

BrainArt for the scientists of the future

The BrainArt SIG is excited to offer BrainArt activities for children during OHBM 2026 in Bordeaux! In collaboration with the Diversity and Inclusivity Committee, we will again participate in the "Brain Mappers of Tomorrow" event, part of which will take place in our exhibition space. Join us for creative, hands-on activities designed to introduce young participants to the brain through art, exploration, and play.

  • When? Monday, June 15th 16.45-18:00
  • Where? Brain Art Exhibition

IMPORTANT: Caregivers must be present at all times during the activities.

Registration information will be posted soon.
